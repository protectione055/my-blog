## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Fullscreen Mermaid Viewer
Readers SHALL be able to open a Mermaid diagram in a fullscreen overlay and inspect it with zoom and pan controls.

#### Scenario: Open and close a diagram
- **WHEN** a reader selects the fullscreen control
- **THEN** the diagram appears above the page and can be closed with the close control, the backdrop, or Escape.

#### Scenario: Navigate on a touch screen
- **WHEN** a reader drags or pinches a diagram in fullscreen
- **THEN** the diagram pans or zooms within the overlay.
